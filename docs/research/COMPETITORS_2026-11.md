# Competitor analysis, November 2026

Researched 2026-11-07 for the owner. Traffic figures are Ubersuggest estimates for US organic
search (domain overview, 2026-11-07), which are models, not the sites' own analytics; use them
for direction and scale. Page findings come from reading each page on 2026-11-07.

tomatoes, best indoor system, hydroponic strawberries, lettuce pH/EC) returned the same groups:

| Group | Examples | What they are |

| Large gardening publishers | epicgardening.com, gardeningknowhow.com | Broad sites; hydroponics is one section |
| Niche affiliate sites | brightlanegardens.com, theindoornursery.com, smartgardenguide.com | Small teams; reviews and how-tos with Amazon or brand affiliate links |
| Brand and retailer blogs | risegardens.com, mygardyn.com, letpot.com, learn.hydrobuilder.com | Content that sells the owner's own products |
| Expert blogs | scienceinhydroponics.com | Technical, research-heavy, aimed at commercial growers |

| Shops and marketplaces | hydrobuilder.com, Amazon, Walmart, Alibaba, Best Buy | Dominate commercial queries |

The Google results page for "hydroponic tower" (Ubersuggest SERP, US, 2026-11-07) opens with an
AI Overview, then two product carousels, an image pack, short videos, a video result and a
"Things to know" product box. Organic results include DIY builds ("15 Steps (with Pictures)",
"Build Your Own ... In 30 Minutes"), a review ("A Brutally Honest Review Of Tower Garden Flex")
and brand pages. Informational and DIY intent still gets organic room; buying intent is mostly
taken by shopping features.

What it shows:

- **The whole niche lost search traffic in the past year,** from the market leader to small
  affiliate sites. The Ubersuggest data does not say why. The results page itself shows one
  likely factor: AI Overviews and shopping features now sit above the organic links.
- **Backlinks did not protect thin affiliate content.** theindoornursery.com has 1,294
  referring domains and is close to zero search traffic; its pages mix hydroponics with
  houseplants, cannabis and bud trimmers, and its reviews show no test data.
- **Epic Gardening still ranks #5 for "hydroponic system"** (18,100 / 46) and holds most
  head terms. HydroRigor should not chase those yet (see the growth plan).

## What the top pages do, and where they are weak

| Page | Strengths | Weaknesses HydroRigor can beat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Epic Gardening, "Deep Water Culture (DWC)" (2014, updated 2025-05-28; reviewed by a master naturalist) | Strong brand, YouTube video, links two extension pages, FAQ | No EC/ppm target, no dissolved-oxygen target, no air pump sizing, no diagram, no tables; unsourced "twice as fast" and "15% faster" claims; 2014 images |
| Bright Lane Gardens, "Hydroponic Tower Garden" (2024) | Readable, FAQ, beginner tone | No plants per tower, spacing, pump timing, pH, EC or light figures; no sources; single-brand pitch (Gardyn via Impact links, $100 coupon); "prune roots" advice without context |
| The Indoor Nursery, "Best Hydroponic Tower" (2023, not updated) | Five picks with specs, comparison table | Claims testing but shows none; manufacturer/Amazon images; contradictions (24 vs 28 plants, change solution every 1–2 vs 4–6 weeks); no prices; no sources |
| Hydrobuilder, "How To Grow Hydroponic Strawberries" (updated 2026-05-01) | Two in-house diagrams; EC, pH, DLI figures | pH and light hours contradict between body and FAQ; no sources; author in data operations; repeated product blocks |
| Rise Gardens, "Best Hydroponic Nutrients for Tomatoes" (2026-04-03) | Practical mixing order, deficiency list, FAQ | "Best" but only recommends its own line; no ppm by stage; pH stated two ways; vague institutional citations; no visuals |
| scienceinhydroponics.com, tomato nutrient comparison (2025) | Detailed ppm tables by stage, micronutrients | No author or date visible; citations numbered but not listed; aimed at commercial growers; no home-scale recipe |
| Smart Garden Guide, "How to Grow Hydroponic Strawberries" (2023, updated 2025) | Amazon-monetized, email list | About 1,100 words, no EC or light intensity, garbled machine-paraphrased conclusion, no sources |

Patterns across them:

1. **Missing numbers.** Most omit EC, dissolved oxygen, spacing or stage-by-stage feeding,
   the figures searchers actually need.
2. **Unsourced or untraceable claims.** Only Epic Gardening links extension pages, and none
   tie each number to a source.
3. **Contradictions inside one page** (pH, light hours, plant counts, solution changes).
4. **Few original visuals.** Hydrobuilder has two diagrams; the rest use stock or
   manufacturer photos.
5. **Testing claims without evidence.** Review pages say "we test" but show no logs or photos.
6. **Single-brand bias** on brand blogs and on Bright Lane's Gardyn page.

HydroRigor's rebuilt guides already beat points 1–4: each number has a listed source,
conflicts between sources are shown, and every guide has two original diagrams. Points 5–6 are
where owner photos and honest, criteria-based buyer guides will matter.

Gardyn's own help page says affiliates sign up at `mygardyn.com/affiliate-sign-up/` and
does not name the network; LinkClicky (2024-03-10) lists Gardyn on Impact, and FlexOffers lists
Gardyn at 16% with a 30-day cookie. Brand programmes (Gardyn, Lettuce Grow, Rise, Click & Grow)
plus Amazon are how the niche affiliate sites earn; none of the pages read showed display ads in
the text.

## What HydroRigor should do with this

1. **Keep the lane: evidence-led informational guides first.** That is where organic room is
   left, and where competitors are weakest. The queue already follows this.
2. **Answer the numbers competitors skip,** in the quick-answer table: EC by stage, dissolved
   oxygen, spacing, pump timing, light (DLI). Resolve or show source conflicts instead of
   printing two numbers without comment.
3. **Win the image pack.** Google shows images for tower queries; original diagrams with
   descriptive file names and alt text that states the numbers are an edge competitors lack.
4. **Video is the gap HydroRigor cannot fill without the owner.** Results pages show short
   videos and videos. A short clip of a real HydroRigor build or grow (Kratky bucket, tower)
   would add the experience signal no competitor page proves. Owner photos have the same effect.
5. **Buyer guides: criteria-based, multi-brand, honest about testing.** When an affiliate
   programme is approved, compare several brands on stated specs, cite what the specs mean,
   and say plainly which claims HydroRigor has not tested. That is the opposite of the
   single-brand and "we tested" pages above.
6. **Programmes the niche uses:** Gardyn (Impact or FlexOffers), Amazon Associates, and brand
   programmes. Add Gardyn to the application list at the network-ready milestone.
7. **Avoid what sank the losers:** mixing unrelated topics, thin review pages, unverifiable
   testing claims and machine-paraphrased text.
