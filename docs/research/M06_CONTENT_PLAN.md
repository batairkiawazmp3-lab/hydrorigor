## Internal discovery plan

Every page receives an incoming link from its parent landing through the typed
M06 manifest. Related crop/system/nutrient pages link contextually to one
another, Start Here links to parent sections, and M04 tools are linked only
where their output answers a real next question. The exact outbound map is in
`M06_INTERNAL_LINK_MAP.md`.

## Cannibalization review

The broad system guide answers architecture selection; the “best” guide answers
beginner use-case fit; the kit guide audits integrated-kit components; the sale
guide supplies a comparison worksheet; and the smart guide evaluates sensors
and failure modes. Crop broad guides, seed guides, and system/stage guides also
have distinct tasks. Verified word-order variants are consolidated rather than
split. No duplicate `/nutrients/` route is created.
