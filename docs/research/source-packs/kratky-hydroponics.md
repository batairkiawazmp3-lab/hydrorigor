## Figures and locators

**Registered: UF/IFAS HS1488 (January 2025)** — see its locator in `m06.ts`: no pumps or
electricity; 5-gallon bucket with three 2-inch net cups; each lettuce plant needs 1–1.5 gal of
water from seed to harvest; about 1 inch of air space; EC about 1250 µS/cm, pH 5.5–6.

**Registered: Kratky, Acta Hort. 843** — all solution added before planting; 3–6 L used per
plant; air gap and oxygen roots; no pumps; starting EC about 1.5.

**VC-1 (Kratky 2010)**
- Solution volume: "Tanks are filled with 1.5–2 gallons of nutrient solution per plant prior
  to planting" (a 50-head tank needs about 75–100 gallons). Suits crops needing less than
  2 gallons per plant for the whole season.
- Start: only the lower ½–1 inch of each net pot sits in the solution at transplanting;
  capillary action wets the medium. As the level falls, a moist air space forms; roots there
  are "oxygen roots".
- Never raise the solution level (submerged roots can drown); do not lift the cover during the
  crop. If the tank will run dry before harvest, add about ½–1 inch of water or half-strength
  solution; if that happens every crop, use deeper tanks.
- Solution: two stocks kept separate until added (A: 8-15-36 fertilizer with magnesium
  sulfate; B: calcium nitrate), mixed to EC 1.5 mS; about 1.2–1.5 mS in hot weather and
  1.6–2.0 mS in cool weather; be wary of EC over 2.0 and avoid over 3.0.
- pH 5.5–6.5; below 4.0 or above 7.0 is abnormal and can stunt or kill plants.
- Cover: ½–1-inch expanded polystyrene sheets, 2-inch holes, painted white, loose fitting;
  tank depth 5½ inches.
- Harvest: leafy and semi-head lettuce about 4–6 weeks after transplanting 1–3-week-old
  seedlings; winter crops take longer.
- Temperature: lettuce grows best at 60–70 °F and tolerates the 80s and low 90s; seed can go
  thermodormant at temperatures as low as 82 °F.
- Crops: leafy, romaine and semi-head lettuce, cilantro, green onions, kai choy, pak choi,
  watercress. Long-season crops such as tomatoes and cucumbers are not suited to this tank
  method.
- Problems: rain entering the tank raises the level and drowns roots; mosquitoes (screen the
  structure and put window screen in the tank below the starting solution line).

**Kratky 2004 (Acta Hort. 648, home bottle method)**
- 4-litre plastic bottle; 7.5-cm net pot; lower 3 cm of the pot in the solution at the start;
  5 g of 10-8-22 hydroponic fertilizer in 4 L gives EC 1.5 mS.
- Lettuce ready about 6 weeks from seeding; the level usually drops 15–25 cm; a 200-gram lettuce
  plant uses about 3–4 L of solution.
- No solution added during the crop; raising the level more than 2 cm after the air gap forms
  may injure plants. Cover or darken the bottle to discourage algae. The bottle method suits
  only short crops like lettuce.
