## Wave use and intent boundaries

- `hydroponic strawberries`, `what are hydroponic strawberries`, and
  `hydroponic strawberries meaning` resolve to one comprehensive strawberry
  guide. The health query is handled conservatively within evidence boundaries,
  not split into an unsupported health page.
- `hydroponic lettuce` and `growing hydroponic lettuce` resolve to one growing
  guide. System selection and seed/cultivar selection have distinct intents and
  separate pages.
- `hydroponic nutrients organic` and `organic hydroponic nutrients` resolve to
  one certification-and-management page.
- `hydroponic tomatoes nutrient solution` supports the distinct growth-stage
  nutrient guide; the broad tomato page remains a crop setup guide.
- `indoor hydroponic system` supports the broad home system page. More specific
  DWC, DIY, smart-feature, kit, and purchase-comparison pages answer different
  decisions.
- `hydroponic basil` was verified but is not one of the workbook's first 25
  records, so it is deferred rather than added outside M06 scope.
