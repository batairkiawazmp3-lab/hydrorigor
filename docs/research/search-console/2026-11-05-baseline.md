## What to measure against this baseline

At this stage CTR is not the lever: published CTR curves only become meaningful in the top 10
(about 1.6% at position 10, 27–40% at position 1; SEO Buddy 2026 benchmarks drawing on First
Page Sage, Backlinko and SISTRIX). Rows at positions 11+ belong on the ranking-improvement
list (stronger answer, intent match, internal links, authority), not the snippet list.

Weekly, record in `log.md`: totals for the last 28 days; queries and pages with impressions,
clicks, CTR and position; pages newly appearing; indexed page count; and which changes shipped
that week. Compare each rebuilt page's impressions and position 2 and 4 weeks after shipping.
