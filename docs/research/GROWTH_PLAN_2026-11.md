## Is the site under-developed?

Yes, for now, in the sense affiliate networks use the word: the domain is days old in Google, has
almost no search traffic yet, and three in four of its guides are short launch pages. Network
reviewers look at a site the way a reader would, and what they see today is 6 deep guides and 19
thin ones. The fix is the same work that earns search traffic, so nothing here is wasted effort.

What "developed" means to the networks and merchants HydroRigor wants is summarised in
"What reviewers look for" below. HydroRigor's own bar, set from those criteria, is the
**network-ready milestone**:

1. All 25 launch guides rebuilt to the content standard.
2. At least 15 new guides from the Phase 2 plan, for 40+ full guides across systems, crops,
   nutrients and equipment.
3. Two to three months of Search Console history showing impressions and clicks growing.
4. Owner photos of a real HydroRigor grow on the About page and at least three guides.
5. A clear identity: the owner's credentials (and full name, if he chooses to show it) on the
   About page, the working contact address, and network payment and tax profiles in the site
   owner's own name.

At five content runs a week (19 rebuilds plus 15 new guides is 34 runs) the content part takes
about seven weeks, so the milestone can be met in the second half of December 2026; reapply to
strict networks in mid-January 2027, when items 3–5 can also be shown. At the current two runs a
week it would take about four months.

## What reviewers look for

From each network's own help pages, read 2026-11-06. None publishes a traffic minimum except
through Amazon's sales gate; all of them judge content depth, identity and reach.

| Network | What it checks | Numbers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amazon Associates | Public site owned by the applicant, "robust original content", recent posts | Account is reviewed only after 3 qualifying sales within 180 days; about 10 posts as a rule of thumb; a rejected application is not reassessed |
| Awin (now includes ShareASale) | Manual review of every application, including identity; URLs and promotion method must be accurate | £5 deposit, returned at the first payment |
| Impact.com | Authenticity and compliance; declines for unverified identity, properties below "quality standards", or "limited reach or activity" | Decision within 72 hours; reapply "once your reach has grown" |
| Skimlinks | Original content with commercial intent, an About page that identifies the owner, "regular, verifiable organic traffic" | 10 original articles; 90 days before reapplying |
| Partnerize / Ascend | No public criteria found | International partners are paid by PayPal only, a US bank, or a Payoneer US account |

Reading of the Ascend denial (an interpretation; Ascend gave only generic reasons): the site was
four days old in Google with 19 short guides, and Ascend pays international partners only by
PayPal, a US bank account or a Payoneer US account, so the payment details in a Pakistan-based
application are a likely sticking point. Both are fixable.

How long search traffic takes (Ahrefs study of 2 million pages, "How long does it take to rank
in Google?"): only 5.7% of pages reached the top 10 for any keyword within a year, and those that
did mostly took about 61–182 days. Low-difficulty topics and steady publishing are the way to be
in that group, which is why the queue favours difficulty below about 30.

## Monetisation, in order

Payment from Pakistan decides the order as much as approval odds do.

1. **Payoneer account (owner, now).** Awin pays international publishers through Payoneer Global
   Bank Transfer to their own bank, Payoneer serves Pakistan, and Payoneer is the reported route
   for Amazon (a USD receiving account entered as US direct deposit; Pakistan is not on Amazon's
   direct-deposit list). Partnerize also suggests it.
2. **Awin (at the network-ready milestone).** Manual review, no published traffic minimum,
   $20 / £20 payment threshold, £5 deposit. True Leaf Market appears to have an Awin profile
   (not confirmed), which would be a second route to the brand Ascend declined.
3. **FlexOffers or Impact for Gardyn (same time).** Gardyn is listed on Impact (LinkClicky, 2024) and
   FlexOffers lists it at 16% with a 30-day cookie; FlexOffers also has listings for
   Click & Grow, Lettuce Grow and True Leaf Market. Confirm its payment options for Pakistan
   before relying on it.
4. **Amazon Associates US (when Google clicks are steady).** Applying now risks a closed account
   because 3 sales in 180 days are unlikely at about 30 visits a week, and a rejection is not
   reassessed.
5. **Impact.com and CJ (later).** They hold the large retailers; brands approve one by one and
   want reach.
6. **Display ads (about 1,000 monthly sessions and up).** Needs an owner decision on the CSP and
   Lighthouse budgets first (see the handoff).

Skip for now: Skimlinks (pays Pakistan-based publishers by PayPal only) and Ascend direct
(PayPal or a US bank only, unless the Payoneer US account route works).

## How guides lead to a purchase

Every rebuilt guide follows the same path, so the pages that will carry affiliate links later are
the ones search already trusts:

1. **Answer first.** The quick-answer box and KeyTargets give the numbers the searcher came for.
2. **Name what to buy, by type.** Each guide ends its setup section with a parts list by type and
   specification (for example "air pump rated for the tank volume, with a check valve"), each tied
   to the source that sets the number. No brands or prices until a programme is approved.
3. **Link to the decision page.** Informational guides link to the matching `/best/` page; the
   `/best/` pages link back to the how-to guides for set-up.
4. **After approval,** add merchants in `src/data/merchants/` and turn the parts lists into
   `AffiliateLink`s with the disclosure, starting with the pages that already have clicks.
