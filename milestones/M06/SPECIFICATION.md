# M06 — Content Wave 1 Specification

Status: READY_FOR_AUDIT

## Objective

Publish the first 25 strategy-defined Phase 1 pages inside the static repository
with traceable research, distinct intent, useful internal links, production SEO,
sitemap and Pagefind eligibility, and commercial claims constrained by the
accepted M05 evidence model.

## Authoritative scope

The read-only `100 Article Plan` worksheet assigns IDs 1–25 to Phase 1. The
distribution is four Systems pages, seven Crops pages, seven Nutrients & Water
pages, five Buyer Guides, and two Reviews. `Publishing Sequence` assigns IDs
1–10 to systems/strawberry/lettuce and IDs 11–25 to tomatoes, nutrients, and
early commercial-research pathways.

## Acceptance criteria

- exactly 25 production pages match the strategy plan;
- every page is non-draft, indexable, sitemap-eligible, and Pagefind-eligible;
- every technical page has visible authoritative sources;
- routes, titles, descriptions, and primary intents are unique;
- contextual internal links resolve and section landings expose the wave;
- crop pH/EC guidance reuses the accepted M04 tool instead of duplicating data;
- nutrient calculator references describe label-dose scaling only;
- named product pages are manufacturer-specification research, not hands-on
  reviews or recommendations;
- no affiliate destination, price, availability, rating, Offer, unsupported
  winner, or first-person testing claim is introduced;
- all M01–M05 gates remain green and CI adds `npm run test:content`;
- M06 remains pending independent ChatGPT audit and the pull request is not
  merged.

## Explicit exclusions

M07, analytics, Search Console, Vercel deployment, production publication,
domain/DNS work, affiliate enrollment, live merchant links, prices,
availability, ratings, product acquisition/testing, CMS/database/accounts, and
articles 26–100 are not authorized.
