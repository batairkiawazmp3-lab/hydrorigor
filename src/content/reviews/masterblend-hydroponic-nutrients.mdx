Masterblend 4-18-38 Tomato Formula is documented by its manufacturer as a water-soluble fertilizer component for hydroponic and other growing contexts. The important beginner point is that “Masterblend” does not describe one interchangeable dose: the exact formula, companion components, water volume, and current instructions define the program.

## Evidence boundary

This guide is based only on identified Masterblend manufacturer documents accessed on the publication date. HydroRigor did not acquire or hands-on test the formula. No independent crop-performance comparison, price, availability, rating, or affiliate destination is presented.

Because the evidence is manufacturer-only, the page can explain documented identity and mixing structure but cannot conclude that the product outperforms another nutrient program.

## Understand the formula and the system

The manufacturer’s 4-18-38 instructions treat the tomato formula, magnesium sulfate, and calcium nitrate as distinct components in a specified preparation sequence. The formula name is not the final mixed solution analysis and should not be used as a reason to omit a documented companion component.

Confirm the package identity before mixing. Masterblend publishes multiple formulations for different applications, so directions for one formula should not be transferred to another.

## Follow the current document, not a copied recipe

The manufacturer’s instructions direct users to begin with water, dissolve the formula and magnesium sulfate in sequence, and add calcium nitrate separately after the earlier components have dissolved. Refer to the current source document for quantities and context rather than relying on a copied social post or an unlabeled scoop.

Never combine dry or concentrated components together unless the current manufacturer directions explicitly require it. Use appropriate mass-measurement equipment for dry materials; do not convert grams to milliliters by assumption.

## Water and crop context still matter

The manufacturer identifies the formulation’s intended applications, but a product page cannot determine the composition of a user’s source water or the conditions of a particular crop and system. Water analysis, crop stage, recirculation, root-zone medium, and measurement practice remain separate parts of nutrient management.

HydroRigor’s [pH and EC reference](/tools/ph-ec-targets/) presents accepted source-specific crop records. It does not turn a target into a Masterblend dose.

## Calculator boundary

The HydroRigor [nutrient mixing calculator](/tools/nutrient-mixing-calculator/) supports liquid doses expressed in mL per liter or mL per US gallon. It does not calculate dry Masterblend masses. For this formula, use the manufacturer’s mass-based directions and appropriate weighing equipment.
